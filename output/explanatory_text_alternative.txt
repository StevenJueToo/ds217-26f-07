This line chart compares the percentage of patients meeting the weekly exercise goal of 150 minutes across four follow-up visits for home-based and center-based rehan programs. The x-axis represents follow-up visits from 1 to 4, while the y-axis represents the percentage meeting the exercise goal. Both programs show an increasing trend, with home-based patients improving from 58% to 70% and center-based patients improving from 57% to 79%. At visit 4, center-based patients had a 9-percentage-point higher rate. However, patients selected their own programs, so these differences do not establish causation. The percentages also include only patients seen at each visit, and random variation or differences in who returned could influence the results.
